## 2. Who We Serve

**Primary:** Adults with treatment-resistant psychiatric conditions or chronic pain who have tried standard treatments without adequate relief.

**Common profiles:**
- Depression patient who has failed 2+ antidepressants
- PTSD sufferer not fully helped by SSRIs or therapy alone
- Chronic pain patient with CRPS, fibromyalgia, or neuropathic pain
- Family member researching options for a loved one in crisis
- Patient referred by psychiatrist or pain specialist

**NOT for:** Patients seeking a "ketamine experience," recreational use, or patients without a legitimate medical history for the conditions treated.

## 3. Core Problem We Solve

Standard treatments for depression, PTSD, and chronic pain fail a significant portion of patients. These people are left with limited options, often years into suffering, after exhausting conventional medicine. Ketamine works through a completely different mechanism — and often works when nothing else has.

The clinic provides medically supervised, properly dosed IV ketamine therapy in a clinical (not spa-like) environment, administered by board-certified physicians.

## 4. Positioning

**One sentence:** Huntsville's medically supervised ketamine clinic — for patients who have tried everything else.

**vs. other ketamine clinics:** Physician-administered (not nurse-only), evidence-based dosing protocol (Berman/NIMH standard), honest about what ketamine can and cannot do.

**vs. antidepressants:** Different mechanism (NMDA/glutamate, not serotonin). Works within 24 hours, not 4–8 weeks. Effective when SSRIs/SNRIs have failed.

**vs. doing nothing:** Treatment-resistant depression carries a significant mortality risk. Ketamine is not experimental — it is a well-studied intervention with 20+ years of clinical research.

## 8. Tone of Voice

**Personality:** Knowledgeable, honest physician. Warm but clinical. Never sells hope — delivers facts. Acknowledges what ketamine cannot do as clearly as what it can.

**For patients in crisis:** Direct, calm, actionable. They've been suffering for years. Don't make them scroll for the answer.

**On difficult topics (suicidal ideation, treatment failure):** Address directly and compassionately. Do not euphemize or avoid.

**Good:** "Roughly 30% of people with major depression don't respond to standard antidepressants. Ketamine works through a different pathway — NMDA glutamate receptors — and produces a response in approximately 70% of treatment-resistant cases."
**Bad:** "Ketamine offers a revolutionary new approach to mental health that could transform your life."

## 9. Content Rules

- Lead with the clinical fact, not the emotional pitch.
- Always cite named research: NIMH, NEJM, JAMA Psychiatry, Berman et al., specific trial names.
- Specific numbers required: "70% response rate" not "many patients improve." "24 hours" not "rapid onset."
- Always include medical disclaimer on articles discussing outcomes.
- Never overstate: ketamine is a tool, not a cure. Be honest about duration of effect, need for maintenance, and the fact that ~30% do not respond.
- Name real Huntsville/Alabama context: Madison County, Cummings Research Park, HudsonAlpha, UAH, Redstone Arsenal area (employer base = engineers, veterans, researchers — relevant audience).
- Flag rather than speculate: if a claim is not backed by published research, say so.
