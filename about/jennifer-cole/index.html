Jennifer Cole

Mental Health & Ketamine Therapy Writer

Jennifer Cole writes about ketamine therapy, treatment-resistant depression, and mental health treatment options for patients in Huntsville, AL. She has covered behavioral health and emerging psychiatric treatments in the Tennessee Valley for over 5 years, tracking how new protocols — from IV ketamine to nasal esketamine — are changing outcomes for patients who haven't responded to traditional antidepressants.

Her writing focuses on helping patients and families understand what ketamine therapy is, what it isn't, and what the clinical evidence actually shows — so they can make informed decisions about treatment.

Jennifer writes exclusively for Huntsville Ketamine Clinic, connecting patients in the Tennessee Valley with licensed providers offering ketamine infusion therapy for treatment-resistant depression, anxiety, PTSD, and chronic pain.
