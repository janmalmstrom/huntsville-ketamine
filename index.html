Conditions We Treat

Ketamine Therapy Works When Other Treatments Have Failed

Ketamine infusion therapy has strong clinical evidence for several conditions that don't respond well to standard medications. These are the most common reasons Huntsville patients seek treatment.

Treatment-Resistant Depression

Failed two or more antidepressants? Ketamine works through a different mechanism — NMDA receptor modulation — and often produces results within 24 hours.

PTSD

Ketamine reduces the emotional charge of traumatic memories. Several studies show significant PTSD symptom reduction after a standard series of infusions.

Anxiety Disorders

Generalized anxiety, social anxiety, and panic disorder have all shown response to ketamine therapy, particularly when paired with ongoing therapy.

OCD

Obsessive-compulsive disorder that hasn't responded to SSRIs or CBT may respond to ketamine — research shows rapid reduction in intrusive thoughts.

Chronic Pain

Fibromyalgia, neuropathic pain, and complex regional pain syndrome (CRPS) respond to ketamine's ability to reset central sensitization in the nervous system.

Bipolar Depression

The depressive phase of bipolar disorder — often the hardest to treat — responds to ketamine without the mood destabilization risk of traditional antidepressants.

The Ketamine Infusion Process

IV ketamine therapy is not a quick fix — it's a structured medical protocol. Here's how the process works from first contact to ongoing care.

1

Medical Consultation

A physician reviews your full medical and psychiatric history to confirm you're a candidate. Ketamine is not appropriate for everyone — we screen carefully.

2

Personalized Treatment Plan

Most patients start with a series of 6 infusions over 2–3 weeks. Dosing is calculated by weight and adjusted based on your response to the first infusion.

3

Infusion Sessions

Each infusion takes 45–60 minutes in a private suite. You're monitored throughout. Most patients experience dissociative effects during the infusion that resolve completely within 30 minutes of completion.

4

Recovery & Integration

You'll rest for 30 minutes post-infusion before discharge. A driver is required for every session. Many patients use the days after infusions for therapy or journaling to maximize benefit.

5

Maintenance (if needed)

Some patients maintain results with booster infusions every 4–8 weeks. Others remain symptom-free for months or years after the initial series.
